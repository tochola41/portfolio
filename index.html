FAQ

Questions people ask.

How long does a project take?

A starter website usually takes about a week. Business websites and accounting systems take two to four weeks, depending on how much you need. I give you a date in the quote.

Can customers pay through M-Pesa?

Yes. I show your till, paybill or phone number at checkout, and for accounting systems I can capture M-Pesa payment messages so sales are recorded automatically.

Will I be able to update things myself?

Yes. Business websites come with an admin panel, and I train you and your staff. If you prefer, the Care Plan lets me handle updates for you.

Do I need to pay everything upfront?

No. You pay 50% to start and the balance when the work is delivered and you're happy with it.

Is my business data safe?

Your data stays in accounts you own, such as your own Google or Excel files. I don't keep or sell your information.

Do you work with clients outside Nairobi?

Yes. I work with clients across Kenya and beyond, over WhatsApp, calls and screen sharing.
